## What is this stream?

Korivash Radio is a 24/7 dark phonk music stream on Twitch. It plays non-stop, every hour of every day — dark phonk, night drive vibes, underground beats.

**Every single song is made by Korivash.** That means:

- No copyright claims on your clips or VODs
- No random mutes during streams
- No takedowns
- You can use this music as background audio in your own streams or videos

The stream never goes offline. If you come back at 3am, it will still be playing.

## Phonk Points

Phonk Points are the stream's currency. You earn them just by being here — no purchase, no subscription needed.

### How to earn points

| What you do | What you get |
|---|---|
| Be active in chat | **+10 points** automatically every 5 minutes |
| Type `!claim` once per day | **+50 points** daily bonus |
| Type `!lurk` | **+5 points** |
| Type `!hype` | **+2 points** per use |

You don't have to do anything special for the automatic points — just chat normally and they build up in the background.

### Checking your balance

Type `!points` — the bot replies with your current total.

## Gambling

Risk your Phonk Points to win more. You can't go negative — if you don't have enough to cover a bet, the bot will tell you.

### Coin flip — `!gamble`

Type `!gamble 50` to bet 50 points on a coin flip.

- **Win** → you get back **2x** your bet (100 points back)
- **Lose** → your bet is gone

Any amount up to your full balance. 10-second cooldown between bets.

### Slot machine — `!slots`

Type `!slots 50` to spin 3 reels with symbols: 🎵 🔥 💀 ⚡ 🌙 👾

| What you get | What happens |
|---|---|
| All 3 symbols match | **Jackpot — 5x your bet** |
| 2 symbols match | **2x your bet** |
| No match | Bet is gone |

Same 10-second cooldown.

### Magic 8-Ball — `!8ball`

Not gambling, but fun — type `!8ball will I win this bet?` and the bot answers. 10-second cooldown.

## Dueling

Challenge another viewer to a direct head-to-head Phonk Points battle.

**How it works:**

1. You type `!duel @username 200` — this challenges that viewer for 200 points and locks your stake
2. The target has **60 seconds** to respond
3. They type `!accept` — a coin flip decides the winner instantly
4. Or they type `!decline` — no points change hands

Winner takes the full pot. Both players need enough points to cover the bet. Only one duel can be active at a time.

## Moderation

The bot automatically keeps chat clean. It watches every message and acts on its own — no mod needs to be online.

**Fully exempt from all filters:** Mods, VIPs, and subscribers. If you're subscribed, none of the filters below apply to you.

### What the bot watches for

**Links** — Pasting a URL that isn't on the approved list gets your message deleted. Protects chat from spam bots and scam links.

*Always-allowed links:* twitch.tv, discord.gg, music.korivash.com, korivash.com, youtube.com, imgur.com, clips.twitch.tv

**All-caps messages** — If your message is mostly capital letters, it gets flagged.

**Spam** — Sending the same or nearly identical message multiple times.

**Too many messages fast** — More than 5 in 60 seconds triggers a soft warning. 8+ triggers a timeout.

**Weird text / Zalgo** — Scrambled or stacked characters get caught.

**Banned words** — Mods can add specific phrases that get blocked instantly.

### What happens if you break a rule

| How many times | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1st time | Warning message, your message is deleted |
| 2nd time | 5-minute timeout |
| 3rd time | 1-hour timeout |
| 4th time | Permanent ban |

**Special case:** If your very first message in the channel contains a link, you skip the warning and go straight to a 5-minute timeout. This targets spam bots.

### If you think a timeout was a mistake

Join the Discord at [discord.gg/VhAj8K4C6F](https://discord.gg/VhAj8K4C6F) and message Korivash.

## FAQ

**Q: Are the points real money or Twitch channel points?**
Neither. They're Korivash Radio's own currency, separate from Twitch channel points. You earn and spend them only within this stream.

**Q: Do my points reset after the stream ends?**
No — points carry over between streams. They'll be there the next time you visit.

**Q: Can I lose all my points?**
Only through gambling or dueling. If you lose, those points are gone. The daily claim and passive earning will always rebuild it slowly.

**Q: The bot didn't respond to my command — what happened?**
Most commands have cooldowns. If someone else just triggered a global cooldown, you have to wait. Check the command list for cooldown details.

**Q: I got timed out but I didn't do anything wrong.**
The link filter can catch accidental pastes. Join the Discord and let a mod know — strikes are cleared easily.

**Q: Can I use this music on my own stream?**
Yes. All music is made by Korivash and is DMCA-free. You can play it in your streams or videos without strikes or mutes.

**Q: Does someone have to keep the stream running?**
No. The stream runs on a server automatically — no one needs to be at a computer for it to stay live. It manages itself around the clock.

**Q: How do I report a problem?**
Join the Discord at [discord.gg/VhAj8K4C6F](https://discord.gg/VhAj8K4C6F) and message Korivash directly.

**Q: What is the Now Playing card that flashes on screen?**
Every time the song changes, a card appears in the center of the stream for 5 seconds showing the track name and a one-line description of its vibe. It fades out automatically.

**Q: How does the scene keep changing?**
The stream has 47 original visual scenes that cycle through in order — each track change advances to the next scene. All 47 play before any scene repeats.
