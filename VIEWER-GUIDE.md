## What is this stream?

Korivash Radio is a 24/7 dark phonk music stream on Twitch. It plays non-stop, every hour of every day — dark phonk, night drive vibes, underground beats.

**Every single song is made by Korivash.** That means:

- No copyright claims on your clips or VODs
- No random mutes during streams
- No takedowns
- You can use this music as background audio in your own streams or videos

The stream never goes offline. If you come back the next day, it will still be playing.

## Phonk Points

Phonk Points are the stream's currency. You earn them just by being here — no purchase needed, no special requirements.

### How to earn points

| What you do | What you get |
|---|---|
| Be active in chat | **+10 points** automatically every 5 minutes |
| Type `!claim` once per day | **+50 points** daily bonus |
| Type `!lurk` | **+5 points** |
| Type `!hype` | **+2 points** per use |

You don't have to do anything special for the automatic points — just chat normally and they add up in the background.

### How to check your points

Type `!points` in chat. The bot will reply with your current balance.

### Ranks

As you earn more points, you unlock higher ranks. They're purely cosmetic — a title that shows up when you use `!rank`.

| Rank | Points needed |
|---|---|
| Listener | Starting rank — everyone begins here |
| Night Rider | 100 points |
| Phonk Drifter | 500 points |
| Neon Ghost | 1,500 points |
| Phonk God | 5,000 points — the top tier |

Type `!rank` to see your current rank and how many points you need to reach the next one.

### Leaderboard

Type `!leaderboard` to see the top 5 point holders in chat.

### Giving points to someone

You can gift points to another viewer: `!give @username 100`

This takes 100 from your balance and adds it to theirs.

## Gambling

You can risk your Phonk Points to win more. There are two games.

### Coin flip — `!gamble`

Type `!gamble 50` to bet 50 points on a coin flip.

- **Win** → you get back **2x** your bet (so 100 points back)
- **Lose** → your bet is gone

You can bet any amount up to your full balance. There's a 10-second cooldown between bets.

### Slot machine — `!slots`

Type `!slots 50` to spin 3 reels with symbols: 🎵 🔥 💀 ⚡ 🌙 👾

| What you get | What happens |
|---|---|
| All 3 symbols match | **Jackpot — 5x your bet** |
| 2 symbols match | **2x your bet** |
| No match | Bet is gone |

Same cooldown as gamble — 10 seconds per user.

### Can I go negative?

No. If you don't have enough points to cover a bet, the bot will tell you. You can't go below zero.

## Overlay Effects

This is one of the coolest features — you can spend points to change what appears on the live stream for everyone watching.

### Neon Rain — `!rain` (50 points)

Type `!rain` and 50 points are spent. For the next 30 seconds, animated neon rain appears on the stream overlay. Every viewer watching Twitch sees it.

### Glitch — `!glitch` (100 points)

Type `!glitch` and 100 points are spent. A scanline glitch effect covers the stream for 3 seconds.

Both effects have a 2-minute cooldown per user. This stops one person from spamming effects constantly.

## Voting on Tracks

You can vote on whether the current song is good or not. Everyone's votes appear in chat.

### Fire, Mid, or Trash

- `!fire` — you think the track is 🔥 fire
- `!mid` — you think it's 😐 mid
- `!trash` — you think it's 💀 trash

Each person gets one vote per song. When the song changes, all votes reset and you can vote again on the new track. The current tally is shown in chat when you vote.

### Rate out of 10

Type `!rate 8` to give the current track a score from 1 to 10. The bot tracks the running average from everyone who has rated it and shows it in chat.

## Moderation

The bot automatically keeps chat clean. It watches every message and takes action on its own — no mod needs to be online.

**Who is completely exempt from all filters:** Mods, VIPs, and subscribers. If you're a sub, none of the below applies to you.

### What the bot watches for

**Links** — If you paste a URL in chat and it's not on the approved list below, your message gets deleted. This protects chat from spam bots and scam links.

*Always-allowed links:* twitch.tv, discord.gg, music.korivash.com, korivash.com, youtube.com, imgur.com, clips.twitch.tv

**All-caps messages** — If your message is mostly capital letters, it gets flagged.

**Spam** — Sending the same or nearly identical message multiple times in a row.

**Too many messages** — If you send more than 5 messages in 60 seconds, you'll get a soft warning. At 8+ messages per minute, a timeout happens.

**Weird text / Zalgo** — Some people paste text that looks scrambled or has tons of combining characters stacked on it (called "zalgo"). The bot catches this.

**Banned words** — Mods can add specific words or phrases that get blocked instantly.

### What happens if you break a rule

The bot uses a step-by-step system. Your first mistake gets a warning. Repeated violations escalate.

| How many times | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1st time | Warning message in chat, your message is deleted |
| 2nd time | 5-minute timeout — you can't chat for 5 minutes |
| 3rd time | 1-hour timeout |
| 4th time | Permanent ban |

**Special rule for brand-new accounts posting links:** If your very first ever message in the channel contains a link, you skip the warning and go straight to a 5-minute timeout. This specifically targets spam bots that join channels and immediately post scam links.

### If you think a timeout was a mistake

If you believe you were timed out incorrectly, join the Discord at [discord.gg/VhAj8K4C6F](https://discord.gg/VhAj8K4C6F) and message Korivash. Mods can clear your strike count with `!resetoffenses @yourname`.

## FAQ

**Q: Are the points real money or Twitch channel points?**
A: Neither. They're Korivash Radio's own in-stream currency, separate from Twitch channel points. You can only earn and spend them within the stream.

**Q: Do my points reset after the stream ends?**
A: No — points are saved and carry over between streams. They'll be there the next time you visit.

**Q: Can I lose all my points?**
A: Only through gambling. If you gamble and lose, that's your balance gone. The passive earning and daily claim always keep rebuilding it though.

**Q: The bot didn't respond to my command — what happened?**
A: There are cooldowns on most commands. If someone else just used it, you might have to wait. For global cooldowns, even if a different person triggered it, you still have to wait for the timer.

**Q: I got timed out but I didn't do anything wrong.**
A: The link filter in particular can catch accidental pastes. Join the Discord and let a mod know — it's an easy fix.

**Q: Can I listen to this music on my own stream?**
A: Yes. All music is made by Korivash and is DMCA-free. You can play it on stream or in videos without getting muted or a copyright strike.

**Q: What does "24/7" mean — does someone have to keep it running?**
A: The stream runs on a server automatically. Korivash doesn't need to be sitting at a computer for it to stay live. It runs, shuffles tracks, and manages itself around the clock.

**Q: How do I report a problem with the stream?**
A: Join the Discord at [discord.gg/VhAj8K4C6F](https://discord.gg/VhAj8K4C6F) and send Korivash a message.
